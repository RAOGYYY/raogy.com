Privacy Policy

BeatFlak — Android game

Mobile Game No data collected Last updated: October 9, 2026

Package name: com.beatflak.game · Version 5.2.0 · Developer: RAOGY · Contact: contact@raogy.com

In short. BeatFlak is a single-player game that plays offline. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no real-money purchases, and RAOGY runs no servers for it. This version asks Android for one permission, vibration, and has no internet access. Your progress stays on your device. We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

1 What the game stores on your device

  • Game progress and settings: cleared stages, stars, scores, shards (the in-game currency you earn by playing), items, unlocks, missions, and your audio, control, graphics and accessibility settings. They are kept in the app's private storage.
  • A random install identifier: a short random code created on first launch. It only labels this device's share of your progress so that progress can be merged without loss. It is not linked to your name, any advertising ID or any contact details.
  • In-game shop receipts: one-way hashes of in-game shop receipts (items bought with shards), which stop an item from being granted twice.

The game does not send any of this to RAOGY or to anyone else.

2 Permissions

The only permission BeatFlak asks Android for is vibration, used for haptic feedback during play. It has no internet permission, so the game itself cannot send data anywhere, and it does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone.

Android may also list an internal permission that the AndroidX support library adds to protect the app's own components. It is not shown as a request to you and gives the game no access to your data.

3 Android backup

Android Auto Backup may copy your progress and settings to your Google account, and device-to-device transfer may copy them to your new phone, under your device's backup settings. The device-only record that holds the random install identifier is not backed up, so a restored game creates a new identifier. Google runs Android backup under the Google Privacy Policy; RAOGY cannot read these backups. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

4 Rating prompt

Once, after you have made some progress, the game may ask Google Play to show its rating dialog. The Google Play Store app shows that dialog. The game does not learn whether or how you rated it.

5 Crash and performance reports

If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google on your device, Android and Google Play may send crash and performance reports to Google. Google shows developers summarised reports in Play Console. RAOGY uses them only to fix problems in the game. The game itself contains no crash-reporting or analytics library.

6 What the game does not do

  • No ads and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No accounts or sign-in, and no cloud save in this version.
  • No real-money purchases: everything in the in-game shop is bought with shards earned in play.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No internet access in this version.

7 Deleting your data

There is no account to close. All game data is on your device, and you can delete it at any time:

  • 1. Uninstall BeatFlak, or
  • 2. open Android Settings > Apps > BeatFlak > Storage > Clear storage. On some phones the button is under "Storage & cache".

To delete a backup copy, use the backup settings of your Google account. RAOGY holds no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side, but you are welcome to contact us with any question.

Clearing storage or uninstalling also removes your progress, shards and unlocks. There is no server copy, so we cannot restore them.

8 Children's privacy

BeatFlak is meant for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9 Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. The game itself transmits no data. Android backups are protected by Google as described in its privacy policy.

10 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, such as access or deletion. Because the game does not collect personal information, there is usually nothing for us to provide, but we will answer any request sent to contact@raogy.com.

11 Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features such as ads, real-money purchases, cloud save or analytics, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before that version is released, and change the "last updated" date at the top of this page. The current version is always at raogy.com/beatflak/privacy-policy.
