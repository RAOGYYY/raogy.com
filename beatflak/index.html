About the game

BeatFlak is a neon vertical shooter whose soundtrack is played live by your fight. Every machine you destroy rings a note in key, combos climb into melodies, and each region's song changes once you free it.

Nothing is pre-recorded: every sprite and every sound in the game is generated in code. Steering is relative drag with auto-fire, built for one thumb, and the whole game plays offline.

Features

Fly through the flak

  • 15 stages across Earth, Mars and deep space, from a coral coast and a city in blackout to storms, ice fields and volcanic rifts, each with its own hazard
  • 15 remix stages with two twists each
  • 10 kinds of Conductor bosses that fight in phases and have parts to break
  • 4 primary weapons that power up as you fly, Nova bombs and 11 special abilities

Build your squadron

  • 7 fighters, each with its own role, perk and signature ability
  • 9 permanent hangar upgrades, one-run boosts and Phoenix Cores for a second chance
  • Earn shards by playing and spend them in the hangar and the shop. No real-money purchases and no ads.

More ways to play

  • Campaign, endless Surge, the Daily Signal and Boss Rush
  • 4 difficulties, from Relaxed to Nightmare
  • Daily and weekly missions, 38 achievements and 50 pilot ranks

Made for phones

  • Relative-drag steering with auto-fire, built for one thumb
  • Left-handed layout and accessibility options: reduced flashes, high-contrast bullets, a visible hitbox, bullet focus and Auto Nova
  • Plays fully offline
