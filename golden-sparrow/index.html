What you can do

Every reward below is calculated and awarded by our servers — never by the app on your phone — and each one has a daily cap.

A curated set of arcade and puzzle games that run offline inside the app — merge puzzles, brick breakers, reaction challenges and more. Your score is verified server-side, so the coins you earn reflect how you actually played.

One curated puzzle a day, scored on the server, with its own daily leaderboard and a separate brain streak to keep. The answer never reaches your device before you submit.

How earning and payouts really work

This is the same disclosure that ships inside the app, under Settings → How earning & payouts work. It is here so you can read it before installing anything.

Ads do not pay coins

This is deliberate, and it is enforced in the database rather than promised in copy. Some screens offer an optional ad in exchange for help inside a game — an extra life, an extra move, a hint, or another turn on the free Coin Wheel. That help stays in the game: it cannot be transferred, converted into coins, or cashed out. You will never be asked to watch an ad to receive a reward you have already earned, and no ad increases the size of any coin reward.

Nothing here is gambling

Nothing in this app is played for a stake. There is no entry cost, nothing is ever at risk, and there is no way to lose coins by playing. The Coin Wheel, Daily Reveal and Bonus Box are free daily bonuses that award an amount drawn from a small, published server-side reward table — the amount varies, but you always receive a reward, you never pay to open one, and you cannot lose anything. Prizes are never funded by pooling players' coins.

Payouts are reviewed, and not guaranteed

Coins have no cash value until you redeem them above the minimum shown on the Redeem screen. When you request a payout we lock the coins and create a request that a person settles manually. Requests are subject to eligibility and anti-fraud checks and may be rejected — in which case the locked coins are returned to your balance. Google Play credit and Google Play gift cards are not offered as a payout method. We do not promise any particular income, and how much you earn depends on how much you play within the daily caps.

18 and over only

You must be at least 18 to earn or redeem. At sign-up you enter your date of birth and our servers work out your age from it — the app on your device cannot declare you old enough. A date below the minimum is refused and nothing is stored. We do not check that date against a document or an external service, so it remains something you tell us and you are responsible for its accuracy.

No rewards for reviews

We will never offer coins, or anything else, in exchange for rating or reviewing the app. Rating us is always optional and always unrewarded.

Under the hood

App

Flutter, single codebase, offline-capable bundled games running in a local WebView host with no runtime CDN.

Backend

Supabase — Postgres with Row Level Security, auth-scoped RPCs, and Edge Functions. Every coin-affecting operation runs on the server.

Sign-in

Google only, through the native Android account picker. No password to forget and no browser redirect.

Notifications

Firebase Cloud Messaging, per-category and mutable. Quiet hours are honoured in your own local time.

Ads

Google AdMob, behind Google's own consent form where consent is required. Ad requests are blocked until consent resolves.

Integrity

Server-side reward verification, per-activity daily caps, device-bound abuse detection, and an audited admin console.
