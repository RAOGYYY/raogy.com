By playing Tinbrook you agree to these terms. They are short, because a free single-player puzzle game with no account and no purchases does not need much of an agreement.

1 The game, and what it costs

Tinbrook is free. There is no advertising, no subscription, no in-app purchase and no paid content of any kind. There is nothing to buy, so there is nothing to refund. You need no account and give us no payment details, ever.

2 Who can play

Anyone. The game is rated for a general audience: no violence, no gambling, no chat, no purchases and no data collection tied to your identity. If you are a minor, playing it is between you and whoever looks after your device.

3. Coins have no cash value

Tinbrook has an in-game coin balance. Coins are earned only by solving cases, and the only thing they can be spent on is unlocking more cases from the archive. They cannot be bought, cannot be sold, cannot be transferred between devices or players, cannot be redeemed for money, vouchers or goods, and have no cash value whatsoever. There is no wager, no stake and no element of chance attached to them: you cannot lose coins by playing badly, only by choosing to spend them.

Because coins live only on your device, they disappear if you uninstall the game or clear its data. That is not a loss of anything of value, and we cannot restore them.

4 Your progress is yours, and it is on your phone

Progress, stars, coins, streaks and settings are stored on your device only. We hold no copy, which means we can neither read them nor recover them. If you change phone, uninstall the game, or clear its data, that progress is gone. This is the deliberate trade for a game that asks you to create no account — see the Privacy Policy.

5 The case of the day

The daily case is the same for every player, and finishing it sends an anonymous score to our server so the game can show you how the day went for everybody. This is the only feature that uses the network. It is not a leaderboard, carries no prize, and is described in full in section 3 of the Privacy Policy. We may change or withdraw it; the rest of the game works offline regardless.

Tinbrook is provided as it is, without warranty of any kind. Every case is checked by an automated solver before it ships, so it should always be solvable by reasoning alone — but if you find one that is not, please tell us, because that is a bug we want.
